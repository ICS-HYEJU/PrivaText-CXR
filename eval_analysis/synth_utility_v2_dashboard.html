What moved the needle

Four paired comparisons isolating one variable at a time. Only real-pool scale produced a reliably large, positive move.

Reading the round

Ruled in

Real-pool size is the dominant lever

Going from 1,100→49,907 real images (same backbone, same epoch count) lifted macro AUROC by +13.6 points (0.672→0.763) — by far the largest move in this round.

Ruled out

Backbone, epochs, and CLAHE all failed to rescue synthetic images

ImageNet pretraining alone barely moved real-only results (+4.0%) and did not rescue synthetic-only training (still ≈chance). Doubling epochs on the full real pool made results worse via overfitting. CLAHE contrast-normalizing the synthetic images left them at chance too (0.501→0.518).

Still open

Is this fixable from the classifier side at all?

Every downstream-classifier-side lever tried this round failed to rescue the synthetic images. That points at the LDM checkpoints’ own generative fidelity as the bottleneck — worth testing against longer LDM training or a different ablation_blocks setting before concluding further.
